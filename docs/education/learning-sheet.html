HELIOS · EDUCATION 1.0 RELEASE CANDIDATE

Explore, explain, inspect the evidence

Use the 3D interaction or its written alternative. Record the claim you learned, the evidence supporting it and an honest limitation. This sheet does not require your name or an online account.

The atlas transforms distances, sizes and time for navigation. Interiors are teaching models. The orange Sun is ultraviolet false color. A convincing image is not proof of an observed surface.

1. Our Solar System

  1. Write the eight planets in orbital order. Mark terrestrial planets, gas giants and ice giants.
  2. Choose two planets. Explain one important difference using the displayed scientific values or sources.
  3. Why should you use AU values rather than measuring screen spacing?

2. Inside the Sun

  1. What produces energy in the core?
  2. How do radiative transport and convection differ?
  3. Choose one interior claim. Is it measured or inferred, and what evidence constrains it?

3. Inside the Planets

  1. Compare Earth's mantle with Jupiter's metallic-hydrogen region or Neptune's volatile-rich interior.
  2. What does the word “metallic” or “ice” mean in this context? What would be a misleading interpretation?
  3. Record one source and one uncertainty. A simplified cutaway is not a photograph.

4. Beyond Our Solar System

  1. Explain what a light-year measures.
  2. Choose an exoplanet. What establishes its inclusion, and which properties remain inferred or unknown?
  3. Why does “confirmed” not mean the rendered surface was photographed?

Evidence checklist

For one claim, record: object and claim; scientific value or explanation; confidence; specific source; source date if known; retrieval/snapshot date; and a limitation. “Unknown” is a valid scientific answer when evidence is missing.
